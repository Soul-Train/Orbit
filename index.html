About Orbit

Keep your life in orbit.

Orbit is here to close the gap between meaning to do something and actually doing it. It is not a taskmaster and it will never guilt you. Things you have not done just get a little more noticeable, and that is all.

The map

Everything lives as a circle. Big circles are categories of your life, and tapping one zooms in to what is inside. Tap the middle circle to back out again. A circle with no children is a single task, and tapping it opens its details.

What the colors mean

  • Green means nothing needed right now.
  • Yellow means it is starting to be a while.
  • Red means it has been ignored long enough to notice, or a deadline is close.

A category takes on the color of whatever is worst inside it, so you can tell where things stand without opening anything.

When and deadlines are different

When is how often something should come around, and it is what drives the color drift. Deadlines are a hard date, and they override everything the day before and the day of. A task can have one, both, or neither.

Importance

Marking something high importance makes it climb lists faster and redden sooner. Most things are fine left at medium.

Getting things in

The bar at the bottom takes plain language. Type something like "add a send letter task to Life Admin due tomorrow" and hit Go. Nothing is saved until you look it over and confirm.

A few other things

  • The daily strip up top is for things you touch most days. Tap to check off, drag to reorder.
  • The two icons on the left of the map show your most urgent tasks and a plot of urgency against importance.
  • The magnifier searches tasks and categories, and jumps you straight there.
  • Tasks can go dormant for part of the year, so seasonal things stop nagging off-season.
  • Nothing is ever really deleted right away, and most actions can be undone.

Import new data

Paste an indented list, or a table copied from Export. An indented list always adds. A table matches what is already there by name, so edits update instead of duplicating.

Spark’s connection

Spark needs a connection to think. Without one, Orbit still files things away, but it can only add, never log or update. The key is kept on this device and never leaves it.
